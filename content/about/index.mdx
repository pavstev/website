## What I have built

I started as a freelance web developer in PHP, with Symfony and Laravel. Then I moved to Node.js, NestJS and TypeScript, and I stayed there. On the way I learned Kafka, PostgreSQL, Redis, Kubernetes and AWS.

I worked in four industries. In fintech I led the backend of a core banking platform and its rewrite into services. In betting I built the event flows behind bets, odds and settlement. In healthtech I moved a medical education platform to AWS. In fleet logistics I am the CTO of Safety Real Time.

</section>

<section>

## How AI changed my work

Since 2024 I build with AI agents every day. I plan, review and decide. The agents write a large part of the code, run the checks and tell me what broke. It did not make me care less about the code. It made me care more about the plan, the tests and the review.

This website is one result. I built it with Claude Code in eight days, including a sky made from real stars. Its story is on [my work page](/work/website).

</section>

## What I care about

I like small teams with clear goals. I like interfaces that say what they do, estimates that are honest, and software that stays quiet in production. I care about the parts people touch as much as the backend, so I learned enough React and Swift to build them.

I like to teach, and I have mentored teams through big rewrites. If you build something and want a second pair of eyes, or a pair of hands, [write me](mailto:hi@stevanpavlovic.com).
