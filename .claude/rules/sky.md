# Sky, planets and shaders

Loads when you touch the sky, the planets, the logo planets or any WebGL helper. Sibling rules: `card.md`, `motion.md`, `globe.md`.

- Logo planets: `product-mark.tsx` renders `span.logo-planet[data-logo]` with an `aria-hidden` inline `svg` (a disc `circle`, then a `g[transform]` of `path[data-ink="mark"|"accent"]`) filled from the inline `--logo-disc`, `--logo-mark` and `--logo-accent`. That flat mark is the fallback (no JS, no WebGL, software renderer, Save-Data, context loss, forced colors, print) and the source the canvas reads, so the path data ships once. `RepoPlanetsCanvas` sits in each drawer's `.drawer-stage` (see Drawers in `card.md`), uses its parent as the stage, boots when its drawer opens and disposes when it closes; `repo-planets.ts` finds `.repo-planet` and `.logo-planet` nodes and sets `data-planets="gl"` on the stage itself, and `@media screen and (forced-colors: none)` hides `[data-planets="gl"] .logo-planet svg` with a 300 ms fade once the first frame (the sunrise start pose) is drawn. `createLogoPlanet` (`src/lib/logo-planet.ts`) draws the paths with `Path2D` into a 256x256 mask (mark ink red, accent ink green, `lighter`; the `<g>` transform applied by hand, as `Path2D` ignores ancestor transforms), uploads it as a texture (no color conversion, anisotropy `min(4, max)`, mipmaps, the settings three.js used before the port) and draws it with one shared logo program: the planet lighting with the mask printed on the front hemisphere only, the logo faintly visible on the night side. A missing color or mask throws, and the whole strip falls back to CSS. Repo and logo planets use the fixed view vector `vec3(0, 0, 1)` (orthographic camera), so a rim does not depend on the tile position. Motion (`src/lib/logo-motion.ts`, pure, tested): a sunrise once per page view from -0.9 rad (turned away, at the terminator, so the mark is in view almost at once) to 0, ease-out cubic, 1.6 s, starting the first time the logo is 60% visible while the canvas runs (one `IntersectionObserver` per logo), and never before the first frame is drawn, so the first frame shows the start pose even while the shaders still compile; then a plus or minus 0.18 rad wobble (period about 9 s, never a full turn), small enough that the whole mark stays inside the disc. Hover or `:focus-visible` in the tile spins it up like the repo planets (speed x3.4, at most 0.28 rad, the `uHot` glow). A held or paused scene, a freeze or reduced motion snaps an unfinished sunrise to the rest pose with one draw; a hidden tab or an off-screen stage resumes and finishes it. Reduced motion draws one still frame at the rest pose.
- Sky (`space-background.tsx`, `data-space-state`: `running`, `paused`, `static`, `fallback`): one sky for the whole site (owner calls 2026-10-14: the blog sky wins, the home page shows it too, only the sun moves between pages; the 100 km view, the Earth, the cloud deck from above, the real BSC5 stars, the planets, the moon, the pointer tilt and the day sky are gone). One WebGL context draws one program, `longRaysShader` (`src/lib/sky-rays.ts`, the clear night `blogLook()` with the LinkedIn cover rays, described under Sky modes). `SpaceBackground` renders the same markup on every page: a fixed `div[data-blog-sky]` (one screen plus `--sky-travel` 40vh) with the canvas and `[data-sky-fade]` inside, and `div[data-sky-sentinel]`. The mode only picks the light source and the ink targets: `blog` has the corona off canvas at the top-left (`cornerCorona`), `home` has the real sun or nothing.
- Sun (owner pick "Real sun position", 2026-10-14): `sunSpot` in `src/lib/sky-sun.ts` (pure, tested) takes `sunPosition` from `src/lib/sky-ephemeris.ts` (Meeus, sun only, within about 10 arcseconds of JPL Horizons) for Vienna at `?at=` or now (crawlers get `crawlerInstant`, a winter night), and places it with the stereographic camera in `src/lib/sky-view.ts` (`skyCamera`, `cameraBasis`, `aimYaw`, `projectDisk`): the yaw puts the sun in the middle of the free side right of the card, or near the right edge when the card fills the width, at its true height. The disk is the true size times 7.5 (`sunGain`). Below -0.833 degrees (sunrise) or off screen there is no sun: the home sky at night is the blog sky with no corona. The shader draws the sun as the corona source with `u_disk` 1: a lit disk (`diskColor`, `diskGlow` 4) with the long rays and no pink prominences or rim (they fade with `u_disk`); `u_disk` 0 keeps the blog and the social images exactly as before. The spot refreshes every 30 s, on resize and after a page swap.
- Text over the sky (owner calls 2026-10-13: no boxes, no shadows): `text-sky` texts with `inkTargets` (home: name 3, bio 4.5, copyright 4.5) or `postInkTargets` (blog header) in `src/lib/sky-ink.ts`, `text-sky` is `color-mix(in srgb, var(--ink), white calc(var(--lift) * 100%))`, and `createSkyProbe` (`src/lib/sky-probe.ts`) draws the sky shader into a 24x6 sample grid per text box through `u_probe`, reads it back one frame later, and `skyLevel`, `liftFor` and `nextLift` pick the lift (98th percentile plus 0.02, steps of 0.05, target x 1.35, raise at once, lower past 0.12). The probe is rebuilt for the new targets after a page swap and skips frames while the shot runs. `?at=` pins the time and also stops the page from counting as a crawler, which keeps test runs on the live sky. Frame pacing: 0.75x (then 0.5x, then 0.35x without clouds), at most 1.4 M pixels, about 90 fps, 30 fps after 800 ms idle, pause when hidden or blurred, freeze after eight frames over 100 ms, one still frame under reduced motion and on a software renderer (`src/lib/webgl.ts`), `createFrameClock` for `u_time`.
- No renderer blocks on shader compiles: the sky program links with `KHR_parallel_shader_compile` and polls `COMPLETION_STATUS_KHR` before the first draw (`whenLinked` in `space-background.tsx`); the planets link their programs with `KHR_parallel_shader_compile` and poll before the first draw (`whenLinked` in `planet-gl.ts`; the context options, the software-renderer check, the clear state, the canvas size, the frame loop and the noise GLSL are shared by the repo planets through `planet-stage.ts` and `planet-gl.ts`, link errors checked only outside production); the globe, the only three.js user (a lazy chunk loaded on its click), compiles with `renderer.compileAsync` and sets `renderer.debug.checkShaderErrors` only outside production. The planet port (2026-10-12) kept every GLSL body, the geometry, the sRGB-to-linear uniform colors, the draw states and order, and took about 104 KB brotli off the first visit (0 pixel diff on the still frame). Measured with `~/powertools/scripts/site-probe.mjs`: no blocking GL calls, startup long tasks fell from 955 ms to 67 ms.
- Sky modes (`SkyMode` in `src/lib/sky-preset.ts`, from the route through `routeMode`): `home` and `blog` draw the same sky (see Sky); `blog` is one sky for every blog page (owner calls 2026-10-13, Q7 and Q17): mounted once by `src/layouts/BlogLayout.astro` inside `[data-blog]` and once by `src/pages/index.astro` (`transition:persist="sky"` on both keeps the island, the canvas and its WebGL context alive while `ClientRouter` swaps the page, between blog pages and between home and blog; the mode switches at runtime), a fixed `div[data-blog-sky]` (one screen plus `--sky-travel` 40vh) with the canvas inside, the clear night (`blogLook()`) with the LinkedIn cover rays (owner pick A3, 2026-10-13: `longRaysShader` from `src/lib/sky-rays.ts`, `coronaSize` 1.35 and `coronaBright` 1.05, with `streamers` 0.9, `fineRays` 0.7 and `raySpin` 0.05 in the shared corona so the rays stay quiet, made by `patchShader`, the same copy `scripts/generate-images.ts` draws the cover with; the home shader is untouched), the corona source off canvas at the top-left (`cornerCorona(view)`, r = 0.43 x the view, where `u_view` is 72% of the screen height, at most 960 CSS px, so the look matches the old 72svh post header), and a shooting star on `cornerMeteor` (y measured from the top of the view, x pulled left on narrow screens), 1.6 s every 6 s, only while the sky runs and never for crawlers. No weather fetch, no clock refresh, no corona layer, no `sky:preset` event, no per-post sky. Parallax runs on the compositor (`sky-drift`, a scroll-driven `translate` of the wrapper to -40vh over the first 100vh; one `requestAnimationFrame` per scroll where `animation-timeline` is missing), never as a shader redraw per scroll frame. A scroll-driven `[data-sky-fade]` layer fades the whole sky to 80% `--background` over the header height and `::after` darkens its lower part, so body text keeps at least 9.9:1. It runs while `[data-sky-sentinel]` (the first 50vh of the page) is in view and holds a still frame below; reduced motion, the pause button, a software renderer, Save-Data and crawlers get a still sky and no parallax (`data-sky-still` for crawlers and Save-Data). Ink targets: `postInkTargets` (equal to `blogIndexInkTargets`), the title and meta line in `[data-sky-header]`. The full description is in the **Blog** bullets "Sky modes" and "Blog chrome" of `docs/architecture.md`.

- Home to blog shot (owner picks 2026-10-14: blog sky everywhere, only the sun moves, 1.0 s, full reverse on the brand link): both layouts load `ClientRouter` through `src/components/page-router.astro`. `SpaceBackground` takes no props: `startSky` reads the mode from the route (`routeMode`) at start and again on `astro:after-swap`, and the effect has `[]` deps, so the persisted island never restarts; keep it that way. On `astro:before-preparation` to the other mode (`linkMode`) the run starts the shot: `tweenPose` in `src/lib/sky-tween.ts` (pure, tested) moves the corona source on an arc from the sun spot to the corner corona in `tweenMs` 1000 ms (ease in and out). The disk keeps the sun size while any of it is on screen and grows to the corner radius only once it is off screen (`offScreen`), so no big disk ever crosses the page; it stays lit until p 0.75 and is dark (blog) at the end. With no sun (night, crawler instant) the corona grows from the corner. The sun spot is taken at the click (forward) or at the swap, once the card is there (reverse). The shot ends only after the swap; a slow fetch holds the last frame, an aborted navigation ends it at once. The page chrome fades out in 200 ms (`data-sky-leave` on `<html>`, unlayered so it beats the `#main-content` transition, the router waits `leaveMs` before the swap, the root view transition is off during `data-sky-leave` and `data-sky-arrive`) and the new chrome fades in for 400 ms aimed to end at 1.0 s (`data-sky-arrive`, `--sky-arrive-delay`). `data-sky-tween` on the wrapper marks the shot and pins the parallax shift and the fade layer (`--sky-shift`, `--sky-fade-now`): each frame eases them from the values at the click to the live values of the new page (read with the pin off), so a scrolled blog, Back into a scrolled post and a scrolled home do not jump. The shot is an instant swap under the pause button, reduced motion, for crawlers, Save-Data, a software renderer, a frozen sky, a hidden tab and without WebGL; pausing or reduced motion mid-shot ends it at once. `?tween=<0..1>` holds the shot at that progress for frame tests. Measured 2026-10-14 at 1440x900 against a direct load: the end frame differs in 0.2% of pixels (twinkle), the reverse end in 0.3%.
