# Sky, planets and shaders

Loads when you touch the sky, the logo planets or any WebGL helper. Sibling rules: `card.md`, `motion.md`.

- Logo planets (the flat mark only: the planet canvas, `repo-planets.ts`, `logo-planet.ts`, `logo-motion.ts`, `planet-gl.ts` and `planet-stage.ts` were deleted 2026-10-15 with the home drawers, and `/work` draws CSS planets and flat marks): `product-mark.tsx` renders `span.logo-planet[data-logo]` with an `aria-hidden` inline `svg` (a disc `circle`, then a `g[transform]` of `path[data-ink="mark"|"accent"]`) filled from the inline `--logo-disc`, `--logo-mark` and `--logo-accent`, with a 1px ring and a soft glow in `--lang` (the product tint). It sits in the product rows of `/work` (see Work page in `card.md`); the paths come from `src/lib/product-marks.ts` and the colors from `productPalette` in `src/lib/theme.ts`.
- Sky (`space-background.tsx`, `data-space-state`: `running`, `paused`, `static`, `fallback`): one painted sky for the whole site (sky rework, owner picks 2026-10-19; spec `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-19-sky-rework-design.md`, it wins over the painted home sky spec; look reference `_ref/sky-rework/`, local only). One WebGL context draws the `paint` and `cloud` programs (see Painted home sky); `src/lib/sky-rays.ts` holds only the shooting star path and its start, shared with `scripts/generate-images.ts`, which draws the same painted night for the social images. `SpaceBackground` renders the same markup on every page: a fixed `div[data-blog-sky]` (one screen plus `--sky-travel` 40vh) with the canvas and `[data-sky-fade]` inside, and `div[data-sky-sentinel]`. The mode only picks the look and the ink targets: `home` is the live sky of the chosen place, `blog` (the blog, `/work`, `/about` and the 404) is a fixed painted night with corona rays from the top-left corner (`coronaSpot`).
- Painted sky (owner calls 2026-10-19; the painted home sky spec `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-19-painted-home-sky-design.md` still holds where the sky rework spec is silent; do not redesign): `/` draws a painted Turner-style sky that follows the real clock, the live weather and the real moon of the chosen place (Vienna, or the visitor's, see **Weather and place** in `docs/architecture.md`), with a band of space and stars at the top and horizon mist; there is no skyline and no Riesenrad (deleted 2026-10-19). `/blog`, `/work`, `/about` and the 404 draw the same programs as a fixed night (see Sky modes). One WebGL context, two programs built in `space-background.tsx`: `cloud` (`paintCloudShader`: cumulus, a higher finer mid layer, thin cirrus streaks and the overcast deck into a texture with one texel per `cloudCell` (2 canvas px), redrawn at most every 33 ms, 2 layers on narrow or coarse screens, skipped when cover is under 0.05 with no overcast) and `paint` (`paintSkyShader`: the sky colors from the table, space band and stars, moon, sun, the cloud texture, corona rays, rain, mist, fog, snow, paper grain at 33%, vignette). Texture units: 0 the ink probe, 1 the clouds, 2 the sky light table (`u_lut`), 3 the moon map. Set samplers with `gl.uniform1i`. The route's programs link first (`KHR_parallel_shader_compile` through `whenLinked`), the other on idle; the shot needs both, else the swap is instant. Positions come from `paintScene(instant, view, weather, place)` in `src/lib/sky-paint.ts` (pure, CSS px, y up from the screen bottom, cached and refreshed every 30 s, on resize, on swap, on a place change and while the weather eases over about 3 s): the sun and moon from `sky-ephemeris.ts` and `sky-moon.ts` (moon within 0.005 degrees of JPL Horizons, phase and bright limb by the phase angle, for the place), the linear view in `sky-view.ts` (horizon at 2% of the height, 0.98 x height per 90 degrees so the top edge looks straight up; by day the sun sits right of the card, at night the moon, blended while the sun goes from -2 to -8 degrees). Space band: the top 24% (`spaceBand`, owner pick 2026-10-20, the same on every screen) blends into the `space` token with stars at 0.95 by day and by night; below it the star gate reads the sky: stars start with the sun 1 degree below the horizon and are full 8 degrees later. Sky colors (C2 "Real light", `src/lib/sky-light.ts`): single scattering along each view ray (Rayleigh, Mie with haze 1.2 times the weather `haze`, ozone, the Earth's shadow), a multiple scattering fill, a blue hour tint and a tone map that keeps the hue, written to a 40 x 24 RGBA8 table that the paint shader reads as `u_lut` in place of the old gradient, plus an ambient color and a cloud light color for the cloud pass; at night it blends into the painted night palette (sun -16 to -6 degrees). The table is built on idle time, never in the first frame: `warmSkyTable` fills the integrals cache in one idle task and the table follows in the next (`tableWarm`, `tableTried` is set only after a home build was tried); each build is guarded (`safeSkyTable`). The six clock palettes are gone; the night palette and the tints still used are in `sky-palette.ts`. Moon (A1 "Real moon"): `scripts/generate-moon-map.ts` draws `src/assets/moon-map.webp` (fixed seed, 512 x 512, albedo on top, height below, under 60 KB; `pnpm moon`), loaded after the first frame and only while a moon is on screen. Lommel-Seeliger light with a small opposition surge, crater relief only near the terminator, earthshine, a warmer dimmer moon near the horizon, a soft aura 0.55, radius 1.85 x the old one. The sharp disk draws in its own small canvas (`moon-layer.ts`, device pixel density capped at 2, `mix-blend-mode: plus-lighter`, `screen` as fallback) and the main shader keeps the sky, the aura and the clouds; a 32 x 32 patch (up to 64 x 64) of the cloud texture around the moon is read one cloud frame late and dims the moon (`u_moonFace` is 0 when the layer draws the face and 1 when the main shader does, and the ink probe draws with it at 1 so the probe sees the disk). If the second context or the map fails, the main shader draws the moon softer. The moon GLSL is one module (`sky-moon-shader.ts`) used by both. The shaders work in the screen frame (`u_res` = canvas width x `innerHeight` x scale, `u_floor` = canvas bottom minus `innerHeight`), not the 140lvh canvas. Sampler `u_clouds` must be set with `gl.uniform1i` (the uniform sink only sends floats). On home the wrapper has `data-sky-home` (also during the shot): no `sky-drift`, the fade layer at 0, `::after` at `1 - var(--sky-paint, 1)`; the shot is one program: `shotScene` (`mixScene` of the live home scene and the night look) is drawn once per frame while JS sets `--sky-paint` (1 - progress) each frame, the home scene is frozen at the click (forward) or built at the swap (reverse), and the sun and moon fade with the mix. The city glow on low clouds at night (the `city` tint in `sky-paint.ts`) stays: it is real sky glow. Wind integrates `drift` x dt into `u_wind`. Weather (`src/lib/weather.ts` and `src/lib/weather-client.ts`, the browser asks Open-Meteo, see **Weather and place** in `docs/architecture.md`) loads on the home only (`weatherAllowed` needs `home`, and `weatherGoal` picks the target: the `?weather=` or preview weather, else the live report, else the fallback on the home, `nightWeather` elsewhere): once when the sky starts on the home on a full page load, after a place change and when a hidden tab returns after 30 minutes. A visit that starts on a night page asks nothing; the first swap to the home asks (once, unless a report is already there). A swap eases `weatherShown` to the new target over about 3 s (home to night to the calm night look, night to home back to the report; instant when no loop runs); `?weather=<clear|partly|overcast|rain|snow|fog>` pins it, `?place=<lat>,<lon>` pins the place, and a failed request keeps the fallback (partly cloudy). The visitor's place (`sky-place.ts`) moves the whole sky: sun, moon, stars and weather. The bubble and the footer picker ask for it (see **Bubble** in `docs/architecture.md`); the sky publishes the moon or sun position as `sky:spot` for the bubble. Dark ink by day (owner pick, accepted risk): per target `skyRange` gives the p98 and p02 samples, `inkScores` scores dark ink (`inkPalette.dark`, about `--background`) on p02 and white on p98, `wantedInk` picks `dark` when it clears the target times `inkMargin` (1.35), else `light` when it does, else the ink with the better contrast; `nextInk` switches only after two reads in a row (one read when it is firm: a read of the goal probe) and only when the other ink clears the margin while the held one does not, when the held one fails its plain target and the other is better, or when the other wins by 0.5 (the first read decides at once, state is reset on retarget). - Pixel clouds (owner picks A3 B1 C1 D2, cells 2, tones 6, 2026-10-20): every cloud is pixel art on every page and in the social images. The cloud texture holds one texel per cell (`cloudCell` 2 canvas px in `space-background.tsx` and `scripts/generate-images.ts`, `u_cell`) and is sampled with `NEAREST` at the cell center (`u_cloudRes`), so each cell is one hard square that slides smoothly with the wind (C1). The mid layer, cirrus and the overcast deck snap to the same tones; rain, snow and fog sample at the cell center (`cellAt`) and fog snaps to the tones. B1: the sky colors, stars, corona rays, sun, moon, mist and grain stay smooth. The sun and moon glow on clouds stays smooth (banded glow drew rings).
