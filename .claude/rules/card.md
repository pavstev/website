# The card

Loads when you touch the card, its strips, the topic panels or their helpers. Sibling rules: `motion.md`, `globe.md`, `sky.md`.

- Order: portrait (154 px, 134 px on phones and on two-column screens under 898 px tall, 128 px there below 760 px tall; a WebGL2 glass bead from `src/lib/portrait-bead.ts` follows the mouse, or the finger after a 320 ms press and hold on touch, where a short tap still opens the dialog and a swipe still scrolls; it magnifies the photo with chromatic edges, a glint and a soft caustic, stretches with speed, starts on first use and stops drawing when it is gone; no bead under reduced motion, Save-Data or a software renderer; no orbit ring around it (removed 2026-10-12); click opens a dialog where the 800 px image grows out of the card photo and stays a circle (no outer ring), with the close button on its rim at the top right; the same bead code (`wireBead` in `src/lib/portrait-effects.ts`) runs over the dialog image with a 64 px radius once the morph has finished, the card bead frees its context on open, and the dialog bead frees its own on close, so at most one bead context exists), name, summary (Light 300, no halo; blur-in, skipped when hydration starts after 1 s so painted text never vanishes), PDF button and three contact links that share one style (dark glass, the repo-card radius `--radius-lg`, a metal ring whose highlight faces the cursor, an iridescent ring, glow and light sweep on hover, one 0.75rem gap; all four get the proximity zoom from `src/lib/cursor.ts` (`[data-proximity]` on the actions row; distance is measured to the button's core segment and the scale is capped so a button never grows past half the gap); the PDF button keeps its aurora and click sequence: it collapses to a 44x44 rounded square, a 2px line runs once around the inside of its edge (an SVG `path` with `pathLength="100"`, the button's corner shape, starting at the top middle and going clockwise; never a circle, the owner wants the square at every moment), then a check shows and the label returns, and `download-ring.ts` holds the `.download-item` slot at its width while the ring plays, so the row never re-flows (below `15em`, 200% zoom on a phone, the label may wrap to two lines); the actions row (`.contact-row`) is a size container, so when its width drops under `13.25em` (the four icons need 4x2.75rem + 3x0.75rem) the icon group wraps 2 + 2 (`.contact-group`, the `ul` becomes `display: contents`) and under `11.75em` the PDF button wraps its label inside the row; it is a container query because em in a media query ignores the root font size the visitor or a script sets, and nothing in the row may be cut off at 160x346, 195x422 or 390 wide with a 200% root font; every button has a tooltip (a `--radius-lg` rounded rectangle like the buttons, not a pill; owner pick 2026-10-12) placed by `src/lib/contact-links.ts`, which weighs covering a button over covering text: it tries above, then below, and slides a tip sideways (at most 35% of its width) to clear the bio and the strip headings (`.repo-heading`, the text only, not the rule) before it covers them; the PDF tooltip (`resume-tip.tsx`) is a glass sheet that feeds out of the button edge like a printed page, with a small paper page whose skeleton lines draw in, and the page count and size read from the generated PDF at build time by `src/lib/resume-facts.ts`), the strips (`card-strips.tsx`, layout E below): the repo strip (heading "N open source projects" from an i18n template; each row is a card with a stretched link: a three.js planet, the name, then one chip row with the languages first (tinted with their GitHub colors) and the topics after (GitHub's topic blue, `--github-accent` on `--github-accent-muted`, from Primer's dark tokens), fitted to the width by `src/lib/repo-tags-fit.ts` with a "+N" button that opens a popover listing all of them (a 44x44 px hit area on coarse pointers); the planets are one shared canvas at about 30 fps with dark night sides, two-tone lit sides, drifting cloud swirls, a breathing rim, rings, bands, a moon or craters, and they spin up on hover; the CSS planets stay as the fallback (forced colors: the canvas is hidden and each `.repo-planet` shows as a 1px `CanvasText` circle outline, so the 48 px gutter is never empty, like the flat product logos); descriptions drop from two lines to one below 1000 px tall and rows tighten below 828 px, both on the two-column layout only; names never break inside a word (`overflow-wrap: normal`), and below `15em` (200% zoom on a phone) the planet column shrinks to `1.5rem` so the longest name still fits; from `40rem` to `48em`, where the chips drop to their own row, planets and logos sit at the top of the tile instead of the middle; the heading rule only takes the space the heading leaves, so it vanishes when the heading wraps), then the products column, then the footer under a faint gradient divider, with no shadow: `© year name` (the name never wraps) and the weather credit "Weather data by Open-Meteo.com" with its link (`en.weather`, `weatherSourceUrl`; Open-Meteo's CC BY 4.0 terms require it, so never remove it while the sky shows live weather), on its own line under the copyright (owner request 2026-10-12): `.card-footer` is a centered column with a 1.3 line height, the credit is 0.6875rem in `--muted-foreground` with the link underlined as before; the strip headings, the copyright and the credit carry no shadow and no shade box (owner 2026-10-13: no dark boxes or shadows behind text) and take `text-sky`, which lifts their color toward white when the sky behind them brightens (see `sky.md`), so they keep 4.5:1.
- Name: set in Geist 600 (`calc(var(--text-display-size) * 47 / 44)`, letter spacing 0.02em; the owner picked it on 2026-10-12 over nine other fonts). The visible letters are `aria-hidden` spans (`--i` index) grouped per word in a nowrap inline-block `.name-word`, with a plain space between the words, next to an `sr-only` copy, so screen readers read the name once. A pure-CSS 5.6 s "signal tuning" intro (blur, scan lines, rose and teal slices, neon flicker) starts at first paint and stays readable after about 1 s. `src/lib/name-glitch.ts` then sets `data-glitch` for a 0.6 s burst every 8 s, rests after 30 s without input and resumes on input; it sets `data-tuned` first so the intro never replays. Each burst is a WebGL tear (`src/lib/name-tear.ts`, raw WebGL2 created on the first burst; the letter canvas and texture are reused and repainted only when the box size, pixel ratio, font or color change, or until the fonts have loaded: the letters redrawn into a texture, torn horizontal bands, RGB split, scan lines, 0.52 s, `data-tearing` hides the DOM letters meanwhile). The owner dropped the axis-morph warp on 2026-10-12: only the TV glitch stays (intro, tear, color split, scan lines). A burst never moves the layout, and a change of the viewport width ends it. `--text-display-size` stays at 44 px down to about 236 px wide and shrinks below (200% zoom on a phone), so the name fits. Reduced motion, forced colors, software renderers and a held or paused scene skip the tear. Keep every flicker under three flashes per second.
- Layout E: `.card-strips[data-strips]` (`card-strips.tsx`) holds the "Opens in a new tab" note once (`strips-new-tab`, passed down as `newTabId`), the repo strip, the products column and the planets canvas, in that DOM, focus and reading order at every width. `.card-strips` is one grid at every width; from `64em` a product tile is exactly one repo tile tall (within 1 px), below `64em` the product rows size to their own tiles. `card-strips.tsx` sets the row counts inline (`--repo-rows`, `--product-rows`, `--pair-rows` = half the products rounded up, `--rows` = the larger list); the rows carry the named lines `repos-start`/`repos-end` and `products-start`/`products-end`, each `.strip` section is a row subgrid placed with `grid-row: repos` or `products`, and each list a row subgrid of its section (`grid-row: 2 / -1`). The grid's row gap is `--repo-row-gap` (set on `.card-strips`); a heading adds `--card-gap-strip` minus it as a bottom margin and the stacked products heading adds `--card-gap-repos` minus it on top, so every subgrid uses the inherited `--repo-row-gap`. Below `64em` the strips stack, repos then products, with product tiles 2 per row from `40rem` (`--pair-rows`) and 1 per row below (`--product-rows`), the repo rows are `1fr` (the repo tiles share the height of the tallest repo tile, `.repo-tile` has `align-content: start`, so text starts at the same place in every card) and the product rows are `auto`, so a taller product line never stretches the repo tiles. From `64em` the card grows to `--container-page-max: 68rem` (the profile block keeps its widths and stays centered) and `.card-strips` has two columns (a `1.5rem` column gap), repos left, products right, rows `auto repeat(var(--rows), auto)`: both headings share the first row and each product tile is exactly as tall as the repo tile beside it, row by row; the products column ends after its own rows (with two products the third slot stays empty). The two columns must not make the card taller than the one-column card was (1280x720 641 px, 1366x768 659 px, 1440x900 820 px, 1536x864 751 px, 1920x1080 891 px, 1024x768 659 px, measured 2026-10-12; within 2 px), and the stacked card fits from 768x1320 (800x1200 and 1000x1200 scroll, as the full product lines make it about 1310 px tall with its padding).
- Products column (`product-strip.tsx`, `<section aria-labelledby="products-heading" data-products>`): the heading "2 products" (`en.products.headingOne`/`headingOther`) with the repo heading's eyebrow and rule, then `ul.product-grid > li > .repo-tile.product-tile`. A tile shares the repo tiles' glass, `--radius-lg`, hover lift, pointer light, focus ring and stretched link (`a.repo-link` to the product, new tab, `rel="noopener noreferrer"`, `aria-describedby` = the new-tab note plus the line). It holds the logo planet (the repo planets' `2.25rem` below `40rem`, so names line up on phones, `2.75rem` from `40rem`, `2.5rem` at `(min-width: 64em) and (max-height: 51.75em)`), the name, a muted "role · period" line in place of chips and one line of text. The meta is two `whitespace-nowrap` pieces from `productMetaParts` (`"Co-founder & CTO ·"`, `"since 2022"`), so it wraps only after the dot. It sits under the name below `64em` (a half-width tile cannot hold "Safety Real Time" and its role on one line, and both tiles keep the same rows) and beside it from `64em`, baseline-aligned (`.product-top > .repo-name { align-self: baseline }`). The line follows the repo text clamp (none below `40rem`, 2 lines, 1 line on short two-column screens) except from `48em` to `64em`, where it is not clamped (the owner wants it in full there; hirista's takes 3 lines in a half-width tile) and its row grows only the product tiles; `--lang` is the product's `tint`, so the hover edge glows in the brand color (hirista orange, Safety Real Time green). No stars, forks or status dot.
- No-scroll gate: `@custom-variant no-scroll` in `globals.css`, `(min-width: 64em) and (min-height: 45em), (min-width: 48em) and (min-height: 82.5em)` (1024x720 and up, or 768 px wide and 1320 px tall, where the stacked card fits). It sets `html` to `overflow: hidden auto` (the card scrolls instead of clipping, WCAG 1.4.12 text spacing; no scrollbar shows at rest because the card fits) and pins the pause button (`fixed`); `#main-content` becomes a flex column there: `padding-bottom` is only `max(--safe-y - 1rem, --privacy-zone)` (the dock clearance), the old extra `1rem` is an `::after` spacer (`flex: 1 1 0; max-height: 1rem`) that shrinks to 0 when the card is tight, and `[data-card]` has `margin-block: auto`, so the card sits where it did and the spacer gives way before a scrollbar appears; the height tiers use 51.75em and 56.0625em (not 51.4375em and 55.4375em) so the next card size fits above the dock in the gaps between tiers; the dock and the `#main-content` padding use the wider dock query (see Dock); `(min-width: 64em) and (max-height: ...)` queries compact the card on the two-column layout only (gaps, portrait, name, bio, tiles), so landscape phones and 768 to 1023 px wide screens keep the full-size card. Every other screen scrolls like a phone, 768 to 1023 px wide screens under 1320 px tall included. Write the variant in the block form only (`@custom-variant no-scroll { @media ... { @slot; } }`): the parenthesised shorthand splits on the comma and emits a broken selector.
- Bio topics: `splitBio` (`src/lib/bio.ts`, tested) cuts the summary around five words, "Vienna" and the industries "fintech", "betting", "healthtech" and "fleet logistics" (`src/lib/industries.ts`, text in `en.industries`), and fails the build when one is missing. Each word is one `topic-trigger.tsx` button that opens its own `topic-panel.tsx` (`popover="auto"`, so one at a time) with a `topic-heading.tsx` (emblem, title, subtitle), the facts and optional children; the punctuation right after a word stays on its line, and `splitCompounds` keeps a hyphenated compound ("distributed-systems") on one line; `compound-text.tsx` does the same for the bio, the repo descriptions and the product lines. On fine pointers a trigger also opens its panel on hover and pins it on click (`data-hover-open="pin"`, `src/lib/anchored-popovers.ts`), but only where the panel floats (`(min-width: 40rem) and (min-height: 34rem)`, or `data-anchored="always"` like the "+N" popover); where it opens as a bottom sheet, only a click opens it. On coarse pointers each trigger gets a 44 px tall hit band (`.topic-trigger::after`, `inset-block: min(0px, calc((100% - 2.75rem) / 2))`, no horizontal growth), and `.topic-word` is `position: relative; z-index: 1` with a `::before` that spans one line height (`1lh`), so a neighbor's extension never covers another word's own line slot and the nearest word wins where bands meet; where two words sit on adjacent lines and overlap horizontally the band is about 35 px (the line pitch is the limit; measured 2026-10-13 at 320, 360, 390, 412, 768, 1024 and 1280 px wide). The word is set in weight 500 (owner pick 2026-10-12: no rainbow at rest). At rest it is `--foreground` white with a 1px dotted underline in an opaque gray (`--foreground` 60% into `--background`; opaque on purpose, because `background-clip: text` paints the gradient through a see-through decoration) and a 1px dark drop shadow. On hover (fine pointers), focus and while its panel is open (`data-open`, set by `src/lib/anchored-popovers.ts`) the text color eases to transparent, so its topic gradient (`--topic-fill`, kept under the text with `background-clip: text`; each topic color mixed 70% with `--foreground`, so the lightest sky behind the bio still gives at least 4.9:1, Vienna's coral the lowest) shows, with a soft glow in the topic colors and a solid underline. Forced colors get `LinkText` with an underline at every state; print shows plain text. `[data-topic]` sets `--topic-a`, `--topic-b`, `--topic-fill`, `--topic-stroke` and `--topic-glow`: Vienna uses its red, faint white, red bands, and each industry two sky tones; the panels keep these colors (emblems, rings, company link underlines). Industry panels (`industry-panel.tsx`) show a round lucide emblem with a gradient ring, the company (a link that opens the company site in a new tab, its hit area widened to about 28 px tall, 44 px on coarse pointers) and the period, both read from the CV feed, and a few first-person facts taken from the résumé (true and checkable only). `industryCompanies` in `src/lib/industries.ts` names each industry's company (167Pluto, Pannovate, Safety Real Time, Evermed); `industryJob` finds it in the feed by name, ignoring case and spaces, spans several stints of one company, and `formatPeriod` prints one year (`2020`), an ongoing job (`since 2022`) or `2024 to 2025`. The feed wins on links; a company with no valid http(s) link shows its name without one, except 167Pluto, which falls back to its LinkedIn page (`industryFallbackSites`) until hirista holds that link; a name missing from the feed fails the build. 167Pluto needs its LinkedIn page as `websiteUrl` in hirista, because 167pluto.com is parked.
