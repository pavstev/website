# The card

Loads when you touch the card, its section buttons and drawers, the topic panels or their helpers. Sibling rules: `motion.md`, `globe.md`, `sky.md`.

- Order: portrait (154 px, 134 px on phones and on two-column screens under 898 px tall, 128 px there below 760 px tall; a WebGL2 glass bead from `src/lib/portrait-bead.ts` follows the mouse, or the finger after a 320 ms press and hold on touch, where a short tap still opens the dialog and a swipe still scrolls; it magnifies the photo with chromatic edges, a glint and a soft caustic, stretches with speed, starts on first use and stops drawing when it is gone; no bead under reduced motion, Save-Data or a software renderer; no orbit ring around it (removed 2026-10-12); click opens a dialog where the 800 px image grows out of the card photo and stays a circle (no outer ring), with the close button on its rim at the top right; the same bead code (`wireBead` in `src/lib/portrait-effects.ts`) runs over the dialog image with a 64 px radius once the morph has finished, the card bead frees its context on open, and the dialog bead frees its own on close, so at most one bead context exists), name, summary (Light 300, no halo; blur-in, skipped when hydration starts after 1 s so painted text never vanishes), PDF button and three contact links (email, GitHub, LinkedIn) that share one style (dark glass, the repo-card radius `--radius-lg`, a metal ring whose highlight faces the cursor, an iridescent ring, glow and light sweep on hover, one 0.75rem gap; all four get the proximity zoom from `src/lib/cursor.ts` (`[data-proximity]` on the actions row; distance is measured to the button's core segment and the scale is capped so a button never grows past half the gap); the PDF button keeps its aurora and click sequence: it collapses to a 44x44 rounded square, a 2px line runs once around the inside of its edge (an SVG `path` with `pathLength="100"`, the button's corner shape, starting at the top middle and going clockwise; never a circle, the owner wants the square at every moment), then a check shows and the label returns, and `download-ring.ts` holds the `.download-item` slot at its width while the ring plays, so the row never re-flows (below `15em`, 200% zoom on a phone, the label may wrap to two lines); the actions row (`.contact-row`) is a size container, so when its width drops under `9.75em` (the three icons need 3x2.75rem + 2x0.75rem) the icon group wraps 2 + 1 (`.contact-group`, the `ul` becomes `display: contents`) and under `11.75em` the PDF button wraps its label inside the row; it is a container query because em in a media query ignores the root font size the visitor or a script sets, and nothing in the row may be cut off at 160x346, 195x422 or 390 wide with a 200% root font; every button has a tooltip (a `--radius-lg` rounded rectangle like the buttons, not a pill; owner pick 2026-10-12) placed by `src/lib/contact-links.ts`, which weighs covering a button over covering text: it tries above, then below, and slides a tip sideways (at most 35% of its width) to clear the bio and the section buttons (`.section-link`) before it covers them; the PDF tooltip (`resume-tip.tsx`) is a glass sheet that feeds out of the button edge like a printed page, with a small paper page whose skeleton lines draw in, and the page count and size read from the generated PDF at build time by `src/lib/resume-facts.ts`), the section row (`card-sections.tsx`, see Sections below), then the footer under a faint gradient divider, with no shadow: `© year name` (the name never wraps); `.card-footer` is a centered column with a 1.3 line height. The copyright carries no shadow and no shade box (owner 2026-10-13: no dark boxes or shadows behind text) and takes `text-sky`, which lifts its color toward white when the sky behind it brightens (see `sky.md`), so it keeps 4.5:1.
- Name: set in Geist 600 (`calc(var(--text-display-size) * 47 / 44)`, letter spacing 0.02em; the owner picked it on 2026-10-12 over nine other fonts). The visible letters are `aria-hidden` spans (`--i` index) grouped per word in a nowrap inline-block `.name-word`, with a plain space between the words, next to an `sr-only` copy, so screen readers read the name once. A pure-CSS 5.6 s "signal tuning" intro (blur, scan lines, rose and teal slices, neon flicker) starts at first paint and stays readable after about 1 s. `src/lib/name-glitch.ts` then sets `data-glitch` for a 0.6 s burst every 8 s, rests after 30 s without input and resumes on input; it sets `data-tuned` first so the intro never replays. Each burst is a WebGL tear (`src/lib/name-tear.ts`, raw WebGL2 created on the first burst; the letter canvas and texture are reused and repainted only when the box size, pixel ratio, font or color change, or until the fonts have loaded: the letters redrawn into a texture, torn horizontal bands, RGB split, scan lines, 0.52 s, `data-tearing` hides the DOM letters meanwhile). The owner dropped the axis-morph warp on 2026-10-12: only the TV glitch stays (intro, tear, color split, scan lines). A burst never moves the layout, and a change of the viewport width ends it. `--text-display-size` stays at 44 px down to about 236 px wide and shrinks below (200% zoom on a phone), so the name fits. Reduced motion, forced colors, software renderers and a held or paused scene skip the tear. Keep every flicker under three flashes per second.
- Sections (owner 2026-10-13, Q3 and Q5): under the contact row one `ul.section-row[data-sections][data-proximity]` (`aria-label` "Projects, products and blog") holds three buttons in the contact links' family (`.section-link` also carries `.contact-link`: dark glass, `--radius-lg`, the metal ring that faces the cursor through `contact-links.ts`, the iridescent ring, glow and sweep on hover, the proximity zoom from `cursor.ts`; tones from `.section-item[data-tone]`): "N open source projects" (a small CSS ringed planet), "N products" (the two product marks overlapped, 1rem each) and "Blog" (no post count; owner 2026-10-13, Q22). The first two are `<a href="#projects">` and `<a href="#products">` with `aria-haspopup="dialog"`; Blog is a `next/link` to `/blog` with `prefetch={false}`, so the home page makes no extra request. The row wraps centered (1 + 2 at 320 px, one per line at 160 px) and labels wrap inside a button; nothing is cut off at 160x346, 195x422 or 390 wide with a 200% root font. `--card-gap-sections` (0.75rem, the same as the gap between buttons, owner 2026-10-13, Q27) sits above it. The actions row is `z-2`, above the section row (`z-1`), so a contact tip that opens below draws over the section buttons, not under them. The old strip headings, the two-column strip grid, its subgrids and row-count variables are gone, so the card is much shorter (measured 2026-10-13: 438 px at 1280x720 and 1024x720, 452 px at 1366x768, 516 px at 1536x864, 586 px at 1440x900 and 1920x1080, 566 px at 768x1024) and stays centered by the no-scroll flex column.
- Drawers (`card-drawer.tsx`, a client component, one per list): `<dialog id="projects|products" class="card-drawer" aria-labelledby>` opened with `showModal`, so the page behind is inert. From the right on wide screens (`min(30rem, 100%)` wide, full height, a left border and a primary glow), a bottom sheet with a rounded top at `(max-width: 40rem)` (at most the viewport minus 2.5rem tall). The surface is clear glass like macOS (owner 2026-10-13, Q26 and Q28): `--background` at 38% with `backdrop-filter: blur(40px) saturate(1.8)`, a 1px inner edge in `--border`, a 25% backdrop, and the tiles inside switch to a light frosted fill (`--foreground` 4% plus a 9% sheen). Everything inside is compact (Q28): a small 0.875rem title in `--foreground-soft`, tight header and body padding, 0.5rem between tiles, and tiles with 1.75rem planets, 0.5rem 0.75rem padding and a 0.9375rem name. A header with the title (the same "N open source projects" or "N products" text) and a 44 px close button (`lucide:x` passed in from the server, so `icon-data.ts` stays out of the client bundle); the body scrolls (`overscroll-behavior: contain`) and is the `drawer` inline-size container. Focus goes to the close button on open and back to the trigger link on close. Escape, a click on the scrim (the dialog element itself) and the close button close it. It slides in over `--duration-slower` with `@starting-style` and slides out; reduced motion and the pause button drop the transition. The URL hash is the state: a trigger click is a plain hash navigation (`hashchange` opens the drawer, so Back closes it); closing a drawer the page opened calls `history.back()`, closing one a deep link opened (`/#projects` on load) uses `replaceState` with Next's history state, so no entry piles up. Without JS a `:target` rule shows the drawer content in the flow. The content stays in the static HTML (crawlers, the JSON-LD in `structured-data.ts` unchanged): the projects drawer holds `repo-strip.tsx` (`ul.repo-grid[data-repos]`, up to 12 repos, `maxRepos` in `src/lib/github.ts`, sorted by stars then last push) and an "All repos on GitHub" link (`?tab=repositories`, new tab, `rel="noopener noreferrer"`, sr-only "(opens in a new tab)"); the products drawer holds `product-strip.tsx` (`ul.product-grid[data-products]`). Each drawer has its own hidden "Opens in a new tab" note (`projects-new-tab`, `products-new-tab`) for the tiles' `aria-describedby`. The planet canvas (`RepoPlanetsCanvas active={open}`) sits in `.drawer-stage[data-strips]`, boots when the drawer opens and frees its WebGL context when it closes, so the home page loads no planet context at all. `repo-tags-fit.ts` refits the chips through its `ResizeObserver` when the drawer opens (widths are 0 while closed), and the "+N" popovers open above the modal dialog in the top layer.
- Tiles: the tile layout follows the drawer, not the viewport. Below a `20rem` drawer the phone layout (planet beside the name, chips and text across the tile); from `20rem` (`@container drawer`) the planet column with the body beside it, the planet or logo at the top, the name and stars on the first line, the chips on their own row (`order: 1`) and the full description (no clamp). Below `15em` viewport width the planet column shrinks to `1.5rem`. A repo tile is a card with a stretched link: a planet, the name, one chip row with the languages first (tinted with their GitHub colors) and the topics after (GitHub's topic blue, `--github-accent` on `--github-accent-muted`, from Primer's dark tokens), fitted by `src/lib/repo-tags-fit.ts` with a "+N" button that opens a popover listing all of them (a 44x44 px hit area on coarse pointers); the planets are one shared canvas per drawer at about 30 fps and spin up on hover; the CSS planets stay as the fallback (forced colors: the canvas is hidden and each `.repo-planet` shows as a 1px `CanvasText` circle outline, like the flat product logos); names never break inside a word (`overflow-wrap: normal`).
- Product tiles (`product-strip.tsx`): `ul.product-grid > li > .repo-tile.product-tile`, one per row, sharing the repo tiles' glass, `--radius-lg`, hover lift, pointer light, focus ring and stretched link (`a.repo-link` to the product, new tab, `rel="noopener noreferrer"`, `aria-describedby` = the new-tab note plus the line). A tile holds the logo planet (`2.25rem`, `2.75rem` from a `20rem` drawer), the name, a muted "role · period" line under it (two `whitespace-nowrap` pieces from `productMetaParts`, so it wraps only after the dot) and the full product line; `--lang` is the product's `tint`, so the hover edge glows in the brand color (hirista orange, Safety Real Time green). No stars, forks or status dot.
- No weather credit (owner call 2026-10-14): the home footer is the copyright line only. The Open-Meteo credit, its tip and the `weather-badge` components went with the live weather (see **No weather** in `docs/architecture.md`).
- No-scroll gate: `@custom-variant no-scroll` in `globals.css`, `(min-width: 64em) and (min-height: 45em), (min-width: 48em) and (min-height: 82.5em)` (1024x720 and up, or 768 px wide and 1320 px tall, where the stacked card fits). It sets `html` to `overflow: hidden auto` (the card scrolls instead of clipping, WCAG 1.4.12 text spacing; no scrollbar shows at rest because the card fits) and pins the pause button (`fixed`); `#main-content` becomes a flex column there: `padding-bottom` is only `max(--safe-y - 1rem, --privacy-zone)` (the dock clearance), the old extra `1rem` is an `::after` spacer (`flex: 1 1 0; max-height: 1rem`) that shrinks to 0 when the card is tight, and `[data-card]` has `margin-block: auto`, so the card sits where it did and the spacer gives way before a scrollbar appears; the height tiers use 51.75em and 56.0625em (not 51.4375em and 55.4375em) so the next card size fits above the dock in the gaps between tiers; the dock and the `#main-content` padding use the wider dock query (see Dock); `(min-width: 64em) and (max-height: ...)` queries compact the card on wide screens only (gaps, portrait, name, bio), so landscape phones and 768 to 1023 px wide screens keep the full-size card. Every other screen scrolls like a phone, 768 to 1023 px wide screens under 1320 px tall included. Write the variant in the block form only (`@custom-variant no-scroll { @media ... { @slot; } }`): the parenthesised shorthand splits on the comma and emits a broken selector.
- Bio topics: `splitBio` (`src/lib/bio.ts`, tested) cuts the summary around five words, "Vienna" and the industries "fintech", "betting", "healthtech" and "fleet logistics" (`src/lib/industries.ts`, text in `en.industries`), and fails the build when one is missing. Each word is one `topic-trigger.tsx` button that opens its own `topic-panel.tsx` (`popover="auto"`, so one at a time) with a `topic-heading.tsx` (emblem, title, subtitle), the facts and optional children; the punctuation right after a word stays on its line, and `splitCompounds` keeps a hyphenated compound ("distributed-systems") on one line; `compound-text.tsx` does the same for the bio, the repo descriptions and the product lines. On fine pointers a trigger also opens its panel on hover and pins it on click (`data-hover-open="pin"`, `src/lib/anchored-popovers.ts`), but only where the panel floats (`(min-width: 40rem) and (min-height: 34rem)`, or `data-anchored="always"` like the "+N" popover); where it opens as a bottom sheet, only a click opens it. On coarse pointers each trigger gets a 44 px tall hit band (`.topic-trigger::after`, `inset-block: min(0px, calc((100% - 2.75rem) / 2))`, no horizontal growth), and `.topic-word` is `position: relative; z-index: 1` with a `::before` that spans one line height (`1lh`), so a neighbor's extension never covers another word's own line slot and the nearest word wins where bands meet; where two words sit on adjacent lines and overlap horizontally the band is about 35 px (the line pitch is the limit; measured 2026-10-13 at 320, 360, 390, 412, 768, 1024 and 1280 px wide). The word is set in weight 500 (owner pick 2026-10-12: no rainbow at rest). At rest it is `--foreground` white with a 1px dotted underline in an opaque gray (`--foreground` 60% into `--background`; opaque on purpose, because `background-clip: text` paints the gradient through a see-through decoration) and a 1px dark drop shadow. On hover (fine pointers), focus and while its panel is open (`data-open`, set by `src/lib/anchored-popovers.ts`) the text color eases to transparent, so its topic gradient (`--topic-fill`, kept under the text with `background-clip: text`; each topic color mixed 70% with `--foreground`, so the lightest sky behind the bio still gives at least 4.9:1, Vienna's coral the lowest) shows, with a soft glow in the topic colors and a solid underline. Forced colors get `LinkText` with an underline at every state; print shows plain text. `[data-topic]` sets `--topic-a`, `--topic-b`, `--topic-fill`, `--topic-stroke` and `--topic-glow`: Vienna uses its red, faint white, red bands, and each industry two sky tones; the panels keep these colors (emblems, rings, company link underlines). Industry panels (`industry-panel.tsx`) show a round lucide emblem with a gradient ring, the company (a link that opens the company site in a new tab, its hit area widened to about 28 px tall, 44 px on coarse pointers) and the period, both read from the CV feed, and a few first-person facts taken from the résumé (true and checkable only). `industryCompanies` in `src/lib/industries.ts` names each industry's company (167Pluto, Pannovate, Safety Real Time, Evermed); `industryJob` finds it in the feed by name, ignoring case and spaces, spans several stints of one company, and `formatPeriod` prints one year (`2020`), an ongoing job (`since 2022`) or `2024 to 2025`. The feed wins on links; a company with no valid http(s) link shows its name without one, except 167Pluto, which falls back to its LinkedIn page (`industryFallbackSites`) until hirista holds that link; a name missing from the feed fails the build. 167Pluto needs its LinkedIn page as `websiteUrl` in hirista, because 167pluto.com is parked.
