# The card

Loads when you touch the card, its section buttons, the `/work` page rows, the topic panels or their helpers. Sibling rules: `motion.md`, `sky.md`.

- Order: portrait (154 px, 134 px on phones and on two-column screens under 898 px tall, 128 px there below 760 px tall; a dither lens (owner pick C2, 2026-10-20; `src/lib/portrait-lens.ts`, a 2D canvas, pure math in `portrait-dither.ts`, tested) follows the mouse, or the finger after a 320 ms press and hold on touch, where a short tap still opens the dialog and a swipe still scrolls; inside a circle of 0.6 of the photo radius (46 px) the photo is magnified 1.6x, cut into 2 CSS px cells (whole device pixels, on a fixed grid, a cell is in when its center is in the circle) and mapped to the 4 `ditherTones` of `theme.ts` with a 4x4 Bayer ordered dither on luminance, ringed by a one-cell pixel outline in `ditherOutline` (`#C9D6FF`) and two cells of dithered halo (`lensRing`, owner pick C1, 2026-10-20); no chromatic edge, glint or stretch; it loads the photo on first use and stops drawing when it is gone; no lens under reduced motion, Save-Data or a software renderer; at rest a one-cell pixel outline in `ditherOutline` sits on the photo edge (`outlineHit`, it does not pulse) and a dither halo about 10 px wide (`haloBandShare` 16/120 of the radius) pulses in `#93b0ff` outside it (`portrait-halo.ts`, canvas `.portrait-rim` 16 px wider than the photo on every side, no layout shift; owner pick C1, 2026-10-20), 15 fps, one still frame under reduced motion, the pause and Save-Data, none while the tab is hidden, hidden while the dialog is open; no orbit ring around it (removed 2026-10-12); click opens a dialog where the 800 px image grows out of the card photo and stays a circle, with the close button on its rim at the top right; the dialog chrome is interior's Lightbox (Phase 3): a flat scrim of stone-950 at 80% (`--portrait-scrim`, no blur, no sky tint), a 1 px `--ui-hairline` ring and interior's panel shadow on the circle, no gold or violet glow, and a 32 px `--ui-panel` close button with a 1 px `--ui-hairline` border, 9 px radius, a 15 px `CloseGlyph` in stone-400 (hover: a 20% white border and stone-200; focus-visible: a `--ui-accent` border and interior's `0 10px 20px -14px` accent glow at 50%, no outline, `Highlight` outline under forced colors), with a 44 px hit area from an `::after`; the dialog photo gets the same outline and halo with 1.5x cells (`dialogCellScale`; a second `createPortraitHalo` in `[data-expander-halo]`, made on open and freed on close, under the close button); the same lens code (`wireLens` in `src/lib/portrait-effects.ts`) runs over the dialog image with a 64 px radius once the morph has finished, the card lens frees its canvas on open and the dialog lens frees its own on close; it takes no WebGL context), name, summary (Light 300, no halo; blur-in, skipped when hydration starts after 1 s so painted text never vanishes), PDF button and three contact links (email, GitHub, LinkedIn) that share one style (the D2 soft tint control look, see Control look below, one 0.75rem gap: each button is a `.keycap-host` (36 px high, 44 px hit area from its `::after`) holding one `.keycap` (36 px, `--ui-radius-key` 9 px, Geist 13 px weight 500, line height 1.5, normal tracking, in `--ui-key-ink` (`--cool-50`), 14 px side padding, 8 px gap, icons 15 px in the same color); hover lifts the fill and the label; keyboard focus is a 2px `--focus` ring in a `box-shadow` right around the cap, no outline (forced colors get a `Highlight` outline), the same on the PDF button and the section buttons; no rings, no glow and no sweep; all four get the proximity zoom from `src/lib/cursor.ts` (`[data-proximity]` on the actions row; distance is measured to the button's core segment and the scale is capped so a button never grows past half the gap); a press (`:active`) sinks the cap 1 px and scales it to 0.97 through the individual `translate` and `scale` properties on `.keycap`, because `cursor.ts` owns the host's `transform` (the Press Depth keycap, its 4 px lip, the tilt and `press-depth.ts` are retired); a parent whose tip shows (`.contact-item`, `.download-item`) gets `z-index: 1` so the tip stays above its neighbors; the PDF button has the four states of the interior Loading Button (`src/components/ui/loading-button.tsx`: idle, pending, success, error): a click runs `useAsyncAction`, which fetches `/resume.pdf` for at least 450 ms (so the state shows) while the browser's own `download` runs, and the faces ("Download résumé", "Preparing", "Downloaded", "Try again", all in `en.card`) sit in one grid cell and crossfade, so the button keeps the width of its longest face and the row never re-flows; a click with Ctrl or Cmd stays native, a `role="status"` line says the state (the idle label is `--ui-key-ink`, pending `--ui-key-pending` (`--cool-400`, `#a3a8bb`; `--cool-375` on the controls at night), success `--ui-key-success` (`#00d492`, v4 emerald 400), error `--ui-key-error` (`#ff6467`, v4 red 400; measured on interior's dark demo, the v3 hexes were a few steps off); the spinner, check and alert marks are 12 px with a 6 px gap, the faces crossfade with a 3 px rise and a 3 px blur on the spring 260/34/0.8, and the state resets after 1400 ms; below `15em`, 200% zoom on a phone, the label may wrap to two lines); the aurora (`pill-aurora.ts`), the metal and iridescent rings, the glow, the light sweep and `download-ring.ts` are gone; the actions row (`.contact-row`) is a size container, so when its width drops under `8.25em` (the three icons need 3x2.25rem + 2x0.75rem) the icon group wraps 2 + 1 (`.contact-group`, the `ul` becomes `display: contents`) and under `10.25em` the PDF button wraps its label inside the row; The first tip waits 200 ms, the next ones open at once while the group is warm (400 ms after a tip closes), and the tip glides from button to button (motion `layoutId`). Pointer down and Escape dismiss until the pointer leaves, keyboard focus (`:focus-visible`) opens at once, the tip stays open while hovered (WCAG 1.4.13), and touch never opens it. The tip is `role="tooltip"` and the button gets `aria-describedby` while it shows; it is a small floating surface (`.tip`, `.tip-surface`, `.tip-body`; the floating look from Control look below, no shadow), `--ui-radius` (8 px), padding `0.25rem 0.5rem`, at most 220 px wide (`13.75rem`), Geist (`--font-ui`) at 12 px (`--fs-xs`) weight 500, line height 1.375, tracking `normal` (the body's -0.005em is switched off in every float), in `--ui-ink-tip` (`--cool-100`, `#f0f2f7`), details in `--ui-ink-2` at weight 400, and a 6 px dot in the contact's tone with no glow. The copyright carries no shadow and no shade box (owner 2026-10-13: no dark boxes or shadows behind text) and takes `text-sky`, which lifts its color toward white when the sky behind it brightens (see `sky.md`), so it keeps 4.5:1.

A pure-CSS 5.6 s "signal tuning" intro (blur, scan lines, rose and teal slices, a flicker; no glow: owner 2026-10-19 dropped the blue and violet text-shadow glows, and the scan lines and scan band take `currentColor`, so they follow the dark day ink) starts at first paint and stays readable after about 1 s. `src/lib/name-glitch.ts` then sets `data-glitch` for a 0.6 s burst every 8 s, rests after 30 s without input and resumes on input; it sets `data-tuned` first so the intro never replays. Each burst is a WebGL tear (`src/lib/name-tear.ts`, raw WebGL2 created on the first burst; the letter canvas and texture are reused and repainted only when the box size, pixel ratio, font or color change, or until the fonts have loaded: the letters redrawn into a texture in the live ink color, torn horizontal bands, RGB split, scan lines, 0.52 s; the shader draws the letter in the texture's own color and the rose and teal fringes only outside it (`max`, not a sum, so no white where they overlap), so a burst shows dark letters by day and light ones at night; `data-tearing` hides the DOM letters meanwhile). The owner dropped the axis-morph warp on 2026-10-12: only the TV glitch stays (intro, tear, color split, scan lines). A burst never moves the layout, and a change of the viewport width ends it. `--text-display-size` stays at 44 px down to about 236 px wide and shrinks below (200% zoom on a phone), so the name fits. Reduced motion, forced colors, software renderers and a held or paused scene skip the tear. Keep every flicker under three flashes per second.
