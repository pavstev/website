# The card

Loads when you touch the card, its section buttons and drawers, the topic panels or their helpers. Sibling rules: `motion.md`, `globe.md`, `sky.md`.

- Order: portrait (154 px, 134 px on phones and on two-column screens under 898 px tall, 128 px there below 760 px tall; a WebGL2 glass bead from `src/lib/portrait-bead.ts` follows the mouse, or the finger after a 320 ms press and hold on touch, where a short tap still opens the dialog and a swipe still scrolls; it magnifies the photo with chromatic edges, a glint and a soft caustic, stretches with speed, starts on first use and stops drawing when it is gone; no bead under reduced motion, Save-Data or a software renderer; no orbit ring around it (removed 2026-10-12); click opens a dialog where the 800 px image grows out of the card photo and stays a circle, with the close button on its rim at the top right; the dialog chrome is interior's Lightbox (Phase 3): a flat scrim of stone-950 at 80% (`--portrait-scrim`, no blur, no sky tint), a 1 px `--ui-hairline` ring and interior's panel shadow on the circle, no gold or violet glow, and a 32 px `--ui-panel` close button with a 1 px `--ui-hairline` border, 9 px radius, a 15 px `CloseGlyph` in stone-400 (hover: a 20% white border and stone-200), with a 44 px hit area from an `::after`; the same bead code (`wireBead` in `src/lib/portrait-effects.ts`) runs over the dialog image with a 64 px radius once the morph has finished, the card bead frees its context on open, and the dialog bead frees its own on close, so at most one bead context exists), name, summary (Light 300, no halo; The 3D look needs `perspective: 340px` on `.keycap-host`; a parent whose tip shows (`.contact-item`, `.download-item`) gets `z-index: 1` so the tip stays above its neighbors. Reduced motion and the pause drop the tilt and keep the sink; the PDF button has the four states of the interior Loading Button (`src/components/ui/loading-button.tsx`: idle, pending, success, error): a click runs `useAsyncAction`, which fetches `/resume.pdf` for at least 450 ms (so the state shows) while the browser's own `download` runs, and the faces ("Download résumé", "Preparing", "Downloaded", "Try again", all in `en.card`) sit in one grid cell and crossfade, so the button keeps the width of its longest face and the row never re-flows; a click with Ctrl or Cmd stays native, a `role="status"` line says the state (the idle label is `--ui-key-ink`, pending `--ui-key-pending` (stone 400), success `--ui-key-success` (emerald 400), error `--ui-key-error` (red 400); the spinner, check and alert marks are 12 px with a 6 px gap, the faces crossfade with a 3 px rise and a 3 px blur on the spring 260/34/0.8, and the state resets after 1400 ms; below `15em`, 200% zoom on a phone, the label may wrap to two lines); the aurora (`pill-aurora.ts`), the metal and iridescent rings, the glow, the light sweep and `download-ring.ts` are gone; the actions row (`.contact-row`) is a size container, so when its width drops under `8.25em` (the three icons need 3x2.25rem + 2x0.75rem) the icon group wraps 2 + 1 (`.contact-group`, the `ul` becomes `display: contents`) and under `10.25em` the PDF button wraps its label inside the row; it is a container query because em in a media query ignores the root font size the visitor or a script sets, and nothing in the row may be cut off at 160x346, 195x422 or 390 wide with a 200% root font; every button has a tooltip: the PDF button and the three links are one interior Tooltip Group (`CardActions` in `card-actions.tsx` is the one island that holds them; `src/components/ui/tooltip.tsx`, its timers in `src/lib/tooltip-store.ts`, tested). - Name: set in Geist 600 (`calc(var(--text-display-size) * 47 / 44)`, letter spacing 0.02em; the owner picked it on 2026-10-12 over nine other fonts). The visible letters are `aria-hidden` spans (`--i` index) grouped per word in a nowrap inline-block `.name-word`, with a plain space between the words, next to an `sr-only` copy, so screen readers read the name once. A pure-CSS 5.6 s "signal tuning" intro (blur, scan lines, rose and teal slices, neon flicker) starts at first paint and stays readable after about 1 s. `src/lib/name-glitch.ts` then sets `data-glitch` for a 0.6 s burst every 8 s, rests after 30 s without input and resumes on input; it sets `data-tuned` first so the intro never replays. Each burst is a WebGL tear (`src/lib/name-tear.ts`, raw WebGL2 created on the first burst; the letter canvas and texture are reused and repainted only when the box size, pixel ratio, font or color change, or until the fonts have loaded: the letters redrawn into a texture, torn horizontal bands, RGB split, scan lines, 0.52 s, `data-tearing` hides the DOM letters meanwhile). The owner dropped the axis-morph warp on 2026-10-12: only the TV glitch stays (intro, tear, color split, scan lines). A burst never moves the layout, and a change of the viewport width ends it. `--text-display-size` stays at 44 px down to about 236 px wide and shrinks below (200% zoom on a phone), so the name fits. Reduced motion, forced colors, software renderers and a held or paused scene skip the tear. Keep every flicker under three flashes per second.
