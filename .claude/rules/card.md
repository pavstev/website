# The card

Loads when you touch the card, its section buttons, the `/work` page rows, the topic panels or their helpers. Sibling rules: `motion.md`, `globe.md`, `sky.md`.

- Order: portrait (154 px, 134 px on phones and on two-column screens under 898 px tall, 128 px there below 760 px tall; a WebGL2 glass bead from `src/lib/portrait-bead.ts` follows the mouse, or the finger after a 320 ms press and hold on touch, where a short tap still opens the dialog and a swipe still scrolls; it magnifies the photo with chromatic edges, a glint and a soft caustic, stretches with speed, starts on first use and stops drawing when it is gone; no bead under reduced motion, Save-Data or a software renderer; no orbit ring around it (removed 2026-10-12); click opens a dialog where the 800 px image grows out of the card photo and stays a circle, with the close button on its rim at the top right; the dialog chrome is interior's Lightbox (Phase 3): a flat scrim of stone-950 at 80% (`--portrait-scrim`, no blur, no sky tint), a 1 px `--ui-hairline` ring and interior's panel shadow on the circle, no gold or violet glow, and a 32 px `--ui-panel` close button with a 1 px `--ui-hairline` border, 9 px radius, a 15 px `CloseGlyph` in stone-400 (hover: a 20% white border and stone-200; focus-visible: a `--ui-accent` border and interior's `0 10px 20px -14px` accent glow at 50%, no outline, `Highlight` outline under forced colors), with a 44 px hit area from an `::after`; the same bead code (`wireBead` in `src/lib/portrait-effects.ts`) runs over the dialog image with a 64 px radius once the morph has finished, the card bead frees its context on open, and the dialog bead frees its own on close, so at most one bead context exists), name, summary (Light 300, no halo; Forced colors get `LinkText` with an underline at every state; print shows plain text. `[data-topic]` sets `--topic-a`, `--topic-b`, `--topic-fill`, `--topic-stroke` and `--topic-glow`: Vienna uses its red, faint white, red bands, and each industry two sky tones; the panels keep these colors (emblems, rings, company link underlines). Industry panels (`industry-panel.tsx`) show a round lucide emblem with a gradient ring, the company (a link that opens the company site in a new tab, its hit area widened to about 28 px tall, 44 px on coarse pointers) and the period, both read from the CV file (`src/data/cv.json`), and a few first-person facts taken from the résumé (true and checkable only). `industryCompanies` in `src/lib/industries.ts` names each industry's company (167Pluto, Pannovate, Safety Real Time, Evermed); `industryJob` finds it in the CV by name, ignoring case and spaces, spans several stints of one company, and `formatPeriod` prints one year (`2020`), an ongoing job (`since 2022`) or `2024 to 2025`. The CV wins on links; a company with no valid http(s) link shows its name without one, except 167Pluto, which falls back to its LinkedIn page (`industryFallbackSites`) until the CV holds that link; a name missing from the CV fails the build. 167Pluto needs its LinkedIn page as `websiteUrl` in `src/data/cv.json`, because 167pluto.com is parked.
