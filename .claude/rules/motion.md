# Motion, dock and privacy

Loads when you touch the footer Motion switch, the dock, the privacy note, the head script or the motion helpers. Sibling rules: `card.md`, `sky.md`.

- Head script: one plain inline `<script>` is the first child of `<head>` in `src/layouts/Base.astro` (`headScript` in `src/lib/motion-pause.ts`, inlined with `set:html`). It runs before first paint. It sets `data-js` on `<html>`, then, inside a `try` so blocked storage only skips them, `data-privacy-ack` and `data-motion-paused` when `localStorage` holds them. Keep it a plain inline script in `<head>`, never a module or a deferred script, so the flags are set before first paint. It runs only on a full load: both layouts load `ClientRouter` (`src/components/page-router.astro`), which swaps pages without running it, and `src/lib/router-flags.ts` copies the live `<html>` `data-*` attributes onto the incoming document before each swap, so the flags survive. The CSP already allows inline scripts. The home to blog sky shot (`sky.md`) is an instant swap while paused, under reduced motion, for crawlers, Save-Data, a software renderer and without WebGL; pausing or turning on reduced motion mid-shot ends it at once.
- Dock (`page-dock.tsx`, an `<aside class="page-dock">` named "Privacy" (`en.privacy.label`) after `main`; it is not a `footer`, so the night footer stays the only `contentinfo`): only the privacy note, centered at the bottom. From `(min-width: 48em) and (min-height: 45em)` (768x720 and up, so the 768 to 1023 px band that scrolls is included) the dock is `fixed` and `#main-content` takes the full height and reserves `--privacy-zone` (`padding-bottom: max(var(--safe-y, 2.5rem), calc(var(--privacy-zone) + 1rem))`), so the chip is on screen at load and the © footer clears it at the end of the scroll; below that it sits in the flow after `main` (a sticky chip covered the contact links on short phones, WCAG 2.5.8). Blog pages wrap the dock in `.blog-dock`, which keeps it in the flow at every size, at the page end under the blog footer (see the Blog bullet in `docs/architecture.md`); the rule is `.blog-dock .page-dock` with a descendant combinator, because the `client:load` island puts an `astro-island` between the two, and the old child combinator never matched, so the chip stayed `fixed` over the blog footer from 768 px up (found and fixed 2026-10-15, measured at 1440x900 on a post: the chip sat on the RSS link; now `position: relative` at the page end). `:root[data-privacy-ack] .page-dock` and `:root[data-privacy-closed] .page-dock` hide it (outside any media query), so no empty landmark is ever left. Print hides it. The fade-in sits on `.privacy-chip-face`, not on a parent.
- Privacy note (`privacy-note.tsx`, a `div`, plus the `privacy-ack.tsx` button): a D2 control chip (`.privacy-chip-face`, `--ui-radius-lg`, the control look from `card.md`, Geist 12 px weight 500 in `--ui-ink-2`, a 6 px `--ui-moss` dot), "No cookies, anonymous stats", in the dock. The chip face aligns its items to the top and its text to the start, with the dot nudged to the middle of the first line (`margin-block-start: calc((1.25rem - 6px) / 2)`), so a label that wraps (160 to 200 px wide, 200% zoom on a phone) keeps the dot beside its first line instead of floating mid-height with centered text; a one-line chip looks as before. It is a `<button popovertarget>`; the panel is `popover="auto"` and the interior popover material (`.mat-pop`, `--ui-radius-pop` 11 px, 1px hairline, 12 px padding, 12.5 px Geist on a 1.625 line in `--ui-ink-2`, the lead line in `--ui-ink`, the mail link in `--ui-accent`, hairline dividers, OK as a `--ui-well` key; the topic panels' scroll fade ends in `--ui-panel`) with the lines from `en.privacy` and an OK button (44 px tall hit band on coarse pointers through `.privacy-ack::after`, the look unchanged). OK stores `privacy-ack` in `localStorage`, closes the panel and sets `data-privacy-closed` on `<html>`: the dock goes at once and `--privacy-zone` drops to `0px` (owner ask 2026-10-12), so the gap under the footer closes; `#main-content` eases its `padding-bottom` over `--duration-slow`, so the card glides instead of jumping. On later visits the head script sets `data-privacy-ack` before paint, which hides the dock and sets `--privacy-zone: 0px`: the no-scroll card is centered without an empty band, the scrolling pages end sooner and `scroll-padding-bottom` drops to `1rem`. The panel is anchored above the chip and keeps at least 16 px from both screen edges down to 195 px wide: `src/lib/anchored-popovers.ts` places it (`data-anchored="always"`, `data-placement="top"`, flips below the chip if there is no room) and it opens and closes with the shared popover motion (Bio topics in `card.md`); before the script runs, or without it, `position-area: top` and `justify-self: center` place it, and `[data-floating]` turns those off. Cloudflare Web Analytics is switched on in the Cloudflare dashboard (automatic beacon, cookieless); the CSP allows `static.cloudflareinsights.com` (scripts) and `cloudflareinsights.com` (connect). The note must stay true: no cookies, no ads, no trackers that follow people. Adding anything that sets cookies needs a real opt-in flow first (Decline as easy as Accept).
- Pause motion (WCAG 2.2.2), the Motion switch (owner pick 2026-10-20, footer redesign; the old "Stop motion" text button is gone, as were the corner and bar pause button on 2026-10-15). One component, `motion-switch.tsx` (`MotionSwitch`, a `button.motion-switch-track.focus-ring` with `role="switch"`, `aria-checked` true while motion runs, named by its "Motion" label; `hint` adds "Clouds, stars and light effects" as its description; `en.motion.label`, `en.motion.hint`), sits in two places. On the home it is the last row of the sky panel that the "Play with the sky" pill opens (`sky-controls.tsx`, `.sky-motion`, with the hint; see the footer bullet in `card.md`), under the place choice: Tab reaches it from the pill, Space or Enter toggles it, and Escape closes the panel with focus back on the pill (native `popover="auto"`). On the night pages (/work, /about, /blog, posts, the 404; owner pick D3, E1) it sits in the footer row next to the copyright (`.footer-motion`, `client:load`, the label visible, no hint; a `:root[data-motion-paused]` rule draws the track off before hydration, as the server renders it on), because those pages have no sky panel and their clouds and shooting stars still move. The switch is a 34x20 px track (`--glass-fill`, `--ui-accent` when on) with a 16 px thumb and a `::after` hit area; it sits under the Time and Weather ranges, which stay visible under reduced motion (the sky draws one still frame per change); forced colors give it `ButtonText` borders and a `Highlight` track. `site-footer.astro` renders `<SkyControls client:load />` in the home footer and `<MotionSwitch client:load />` in the night footers; never render either without a `client:` directive, or the pill and the switch stay dead. `.sky-controls` and `.footer-motion` are hidden without JS (no `data-js`) and in print, and `.sky-motion` (the panel row) and `.footer-motion` are hidden under OS reduced motion, where nothing is left to pause; those are the only rules without a paused twin, as the switch must stay to resume. No corner button, no bar slot use, no `#main-content` top padding for it. State: `html[data-motion-paused]` plus `localStorage["motion-paused"] = "1"`, removed on resume; storage errors make it session-only, and a `storage` event makes other tabs follow. `setMotionPaused` sets both, then dispatches `scene:hold` and `motion:pause`; the Motion switch reads it through `useSyncExternalStore(subscribeMotionPause, isMotionPaused, () => false)`. `scene-hold.ts` owns the attribute name and `isSceneHeld()` is "held or paused", so a `holdScene(false)` cannot undo a pause; `motion-pause.ts` imports from `scene-hold.ts`, never the other way (a cycle fails `import-x/no-cycle` and knip). Canvases (sky, name tear) stop through `isSceneHeld()` and keep their last frame, and the sky reports `paused`. While held or paused the sky also ignores pointer moves (star parallax, gusts, puffs), so nothing swings or plays on resume; the sky has no click effect (the owner dropped the click ripple on 2026-10-13). DOM loops (`name-glitch`, `cursor`, `contact-links`, `intro`, `portrait-effects`, `portrait-expand`) take their reduced-motion path through `stillQuery()`, an object shaped like a `MediaQueryList` that matches reduced motion or the pause and fires `change` for either. React parts take the same through `useStill()` (`src/lib/use-still.ts`): the tooltips, the PDF button's faces and the blog TOC drawer skip their springs and fades when it is true. `space-background.tsx` keeps `reducedMotionQuery`. CSS: `@custom-variant still` (reduced motion, or `:root[data-motion-paused] &`) is used only as `@variant still` nested in a rule with one selector and no pseudo-element (a top-level one emits `:scope`, which never matches, and a selector list loses its pseudo-elements). Every other reduced-motion rule, the universal `0.01ms` reset included, has a hand-written `:root[data-motion-paused]` twin right after it; the only rules without one are the `.sky-controls`, `.sky-motion` and `.footer-motion` hides, as the switch must stay to resume. The pill shine hide (`.sky-trigger-shine`) has its paused twin, and so does the pill icon turn (`.sky-trigger-icon`, `sky-icon-turn`). The card tips' `.tip-line` draw (résumé, About, Work and Blog) uses `@variant still`. The TOC drawer's slide runs in `motion` and stops through `useStill()`, so it has no CSS rule and no paused twin. After a CSS change, the built CSS must hold no `:scope` and a paused twin for every other reduced-motion selector.
- A still sky (Motion switch, reduced motion) draws snow and rain thin, soft and clear of the card text; see Still snow and rain in `sky.md`.
- `prefers-reduced-motion`: the intro exits early; sky, portrait effects and cursor scripts also follow the setting when it changes at runtime; CSS animations collapse to `0.01ms`. The Motion switch reuses these paths (see Pause motion). Pointer-following effects (star parallax, cloud wind) run only for `(hover: hover) and (pointer: fine)`; the portrait dither lens is the one exception, as it also follows a finger after a press and hold. The portrait dither halo (`portrait-halo.ts`, a 2D canvas) pulses at 15 fps from a timer, stops while the tab is hidden, and draws one still frame under reduced motion, the pause and Save-Data.
- Start order: no planet canvas is left (the `/work` planets are CSS, see `card.md`). The name tear engine starts on idle without waiting for `load` (`scheduleIdle`); on coarse pointers (`isCoarseDevice` in `src/lib/idle.ts`, the inverse of `finePointerQuery`) it goes through `scheduleStaggered`, which waits for `load` and runs on an idle period. The portrait lens loads its photo on the first hover or long press, never at mount.
- Popover guards (a11y review 2026-10-20): in sheet mode (not floating) `anchored-popovers.ts` swallows the click that follows a tap outside an open panel for 450 ms (document capture `pointerdown` arms it, capture `click` cancels it, as `portrait-effects.ts` does), so closing a sheet by its backdrop never taps the résumé pill, the portrait or a link under it. A panel that holds `[data-initial-focus]` takes focus there when a click or Enter opens it; the sky panel marks its first place option. The sky panel closes when focus leaves it for anything but the pill. The privacy panel is a named `role="dialog"` (`en.privacy.label`) and its chip has `aria-haspopup="dialog"`. `.sky-range` and its scale use `touch-action: none`, so a steep diagonal drag keeps moving the value.
