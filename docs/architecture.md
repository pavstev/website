# Architecture

The long Tech Stack notes, moved out of `CLAUDE.md` so they do not load on every turn. Read the bullet for the area you change.

## Tech Stack

- **Framework:** Astro 7, `output: "static"`, `build.format: "file"`, prerendered to `dist/` (`astro.config.mjs`; React 19 islands through `@astrojs/react`; Tailwind v4 through `@tailwindcss/vite`). `build.format: "file"` writes `/blog` as `dist/blog.html` and `/blog/<slug>` as `dist/blog/<slug>.html`; Cloudflare serves them without the `.html` suffix. Routes: `/` (`src/pages/index.astro`, layout `src/layouts/Base.astro`), 404 (`src/pages/404.astro` → `dist/404.html`, `noindex`, on `BlogLayout` with the shared bar and sky, a "Lost in space" heading and links to Home, About, Work and Blog; a small script writes the missing path into the text; `notFoundMetadata` has no canonical and no `og:url`), `/work` (`src/pages/work.astro` → `dist/work.html`, the open source projects and the products as blog-style rows, see **Work and About** below), `/about` (`src/pages/about.astro` → `dist/about.html`, text from `content/about/index.mdx`), `/sitemap.xml` (`src/pages/sitemap.xml.ts`, with image entries; `lastmod` is the committed CV's `updatedAt`, the time the résumé was last edited, never the build time), `/robots.txt`, `/manifest.webmanifest` (`src/pages/manifest.webmanifest.ts`), `/llms.txt` (`src/pages/llms.txt.ts`), `/cv.json` (`src/pages/cv.json.ts`), `/resume.pdf` (`src/pages/resume.pdf.ts`), and the blog (built only while at least one post is live, see `blogLive` in `.claude/rules/blog.md`): `/blog`, `/blog/<slug>`, `/blog/feed.xml`, `/blog/feed.json` and `/blog/<slug>.md` (see **Blog** below; the sitemap's blog entries use the post dates, not the CV's). The home page, `/sitemap.xml`, `/llms.txt`, `/cv.json` and `/resume.pdf` are built from `personal.ts`, `products.ts`, `i18n.ts`, the build-time repo list and the CV file `src/data/cv.json`. The home page stays one card with three buttons (About, Work, Blog); the other pages are About, Work, the blog and the 404.
- **Prefetch and Cloudflare Speed Brain:** `prefetch: false` in `astro.config.mjs` turns Astro's own prefetch off, and `src/lib/link-prefetch.ts` (started from `page-router.astro`, one set of delegated listeners for the whole session) does it instead. Reason, measured 2026-10-15: Astro prefetches with `<link rel=prefetch>`, and Chrome sends `Sec-Purpose: prefetch` for it. Speed Brain is on by default on the Free plan, and it answers that header with `503` and `cf-speculation-refused: prefetch refused: disabled for worker requests`, for every path, because the site is served by a Worker (static assets). The page still worked, but the prefetch never landed and the console showed "Failed to load resource: 503". Speed Brain cannot work for this site: its docs say it skips pages served through a Worker, and there is no per-path off switch (only the zone toggle in Speed, Settings, Content Optimization). A `Sec-Purpose: prefetch;prerender` request is served (200), but `ClientRouter` intercepts the click and fetches, so a prerender is never used and would run the sky for nothing. `link-prefetch.ts` warms the page with a plain low-priority `fetch()` (no `Sec-Purpose`, so the edge answers 200 from cache) on hover (80 ms delay), focus, touch and mouse down, for same-origin links without a file extension, skipping the current page, `target`, `download`, `data-astro-prefetch="false"` and Save-Data. `ClientRouter` then reuses the cached copy: the HTML stays `max-age=0, must-revalidate` with an ETag, so a later fetch is a cheap revalidation and never serves stale HTML after a deploy. A longer `max-age` gained nothing (282 against 294 ms, 5 runs) and was rejected. Home to Blog click to paint, Chrome, 4x CPU and slow 4G, median of 5: 399 ms without a prefetch, 280 ms with the `fetch()` prefetch, live. Speed Brain's own mouse-down speculation got a `503` on every click (a failed request in the Network tab, no console error) until the owner switched Speed Brain off. Checked 2026-10-15: nothing in the repo could remove it, and nothing can make Speed Brain work here. (1) The Worker is the blocker, not the cache: every path answers `disabled for worker requests`, pages that show `cf-cache-status: HIT` included, and `/cdn-cgi/speculation` answers `not eligible`; any `Sec-Purpose: prefetch` request gets the `503` while the zone toggle is on, so a Cache Rule does not help and an own `Speculation-Rules` header in `public/_headers` (Speed Brain does not replace one) could only ask for nothing. (2) Chrome keeps a speculation prefetch for browser navigations only, never for single page app style fetches (Chrome for Developers, "Guide to implementing speculation rules"), so even a host where Speed Brain works would not feed `ClientRouter`; `link-prefetch.ts` is the right tool, and moving to Pages would gain nothing. (3) Switching the rules off does not make a click faster: 22 paired live runs (Chrome, 4x CPU, slow 4G, desktop, Home to Blog click until the blog title is on screen, order alternated): rules on, median 659 ms with a `503` on every click; the rules file replaced by an empty one, median 690 ms; paired difference -43 ms, off faster in 8 of 22. The owner switched the toggle off on 2026-10-15 (account `Pavlovicmstevan@gmail.com's Account`, zone `stevanpavlovic.com`, Speed, Settings, Content Optimization, Speed Brain): the `Speculation-Rules` header is gone, `Sec-Purpose: prefetch` answers `200`, and 14 live real-click runs show no `503`, no console error and a median of 627 ms (same script). Keep `link-prefetch.ts`. With Speed Brain off, Astro's own `<link rel=prefetch>` gets a `200` too, and it tied with `link-prefetch.ts` (2026-10-15: two local `wrangler dev` builds, one with `prefetch: false` plus `link-prefetch.ts`, one with Astro's default, Chrome, 4x CPU, slow 4G, Home to Blog click until the blog title is on screen, 14 paired runs per hover pause, medians): 300 ms hover 386 against 401 ms, 120 ms hover 416 against 416 ms; with no hover pause Astro's own made no prefetch before the click and was 28 ms faster (560 against 592 ms), a case no real pointer produces. The plain `fetch()` stays because it keeps working if Speed Brain is ever switched back on, when the `503` would hit Astro's `<link rel=prefetch>` again. `/cdn-cgi/speculation` still answered `200` an hour later (a cached copy, nothing points to it). Switching the toggle back on brings the `503` back. Sources and the curl matrix: `~/.claude/research/2026-10-15-astro-optimizations/speed-brain-workers-static-assets.md`.
- **Dev:** `devToolbar: { enabled: false }` in `astro.config.mjs`, so `astro dev` shows no Astro toolbar. `vite.optimizeDeps.include: ["three"]` in `astro.config.mjs` keeps `astro dev` off a stale dependency cache when the lazy globe chunk loads three.js. `pnpm dev` runs the blog prebuild with drafts, then `astro dev --port 4321`.
- **UI:** Tailwind v4 (`@theme inline` + `@utility`, via `@tailwindcss/vite`). `globals.css` starts with `@import "tailwindcss" source(none)` and `@source "../"`, so Tailwind scans only `src/` and never `.agents/`, `dist/` or old worktrees. `postcss.config.mjs` (plain ESM, not run through Node's type stripping, so it stays `.mjs`) holds only `scripts/postcss-color-mix.mjs`, which Vite runs on the CSS after the Tailwind plugin has produced it: Tailwind puts each `color-mix()` rule in a `@supports (color: color-mix(in lab, red, red))` block after a fallback rule; the plugin drops the fallback declarations and unwraps the block, so every browser gets the `color-mix()` value and a browser without `color-mix()` (Chrome before 111, Safari before 16.2) loses those colors. ESLint ignores the `.mjs` config files (the typed rules need type information they cannot give). Astro components render to HTML at build time and ship no JavaScript; only React islands (hydrated with a `client:` directive) run in the browser: the sky (`space-background.tsx`), the PDF button, the portrait dialog, the footer islands in the static `site-footer.astro` (`sky-controls.tsx` on the home; `motion-switch.tsx` and `footer-contacts.tsx` on the night pages), the privacy OK button (`privacy-ack.tsx`) and `effects-init.tsx`. `astro.config.mjs` sets `build.inlineStylesheets: "always"`: each page's stylesheet is inlined into its HTML. The CSS is split per route: `src/styles/globals.css` is the shared sheet (it imports `tokens.css`, every color, see `docs/theming.md`; then `@theme`, `@utility`, fonts, base, the sky, the materials, keycaps, tips, drawers, popovers, dock, privacy chip, footer motion toggle); `card.css` and `blog.css` hold the rules only the card or only the blog layout (posts, listings, `/work` and `/about`) can match, and each starts with `@import "./globals.css"` so Tailwind compiles one sheet per page and the rule order inside it stays the order of the files. Each page imports exactly one entry: `index.astro` imports `card.css`, `BlogLayout.astro` imports `blog.css`, `404.astro` imports `globals.css`, and `Base.astro` imports none. Never import `globals.css` next to a page sheet: Astro orders separate CSS modules by import depth, not by the order of the `import` lines (a first try put the page sheet before the shared one and broke the layer order). A new rule goes to `card.css` or `blog.css` only when every class in its selector is used by that one route (a `.a, .b` list may be split when the parts differ), and stays in `globals.css` when both routes use a class; keep a card or blog rule in `globals.css` when a shared rule of equal specificity that comes later in the file sets the same property on the same element, as moving it would flip the winner. The split took the home HTML from 184 KB to 155 KB, a post from 195 KB to 157 KB and `/blog` from 139 KB to 101 KB (`pnpm build`, 2026-10-15). Every page still carries all the Tailwind utilities, because one `@source "../"` scans the whole of `src/` (about 10 KB; splitting that per route is the next cut). `@source not` leaves out tests and `src/profile`, so stray words in them make no utilities. The inlining cut the first text paint from 580 to 330 ms and the first sky draw from 1.81 to 1.56 s on the real throttled probe (`~/powertools/scripts/first-paint-ab.mjs`, 4x CPU, slow 4G, HTTP/2, 2026-10-14). The small `island-style-to-css` integration in the same file removes the invalid `<style>astro-island,...` that Astro's island runtime writes into the body (the audit's html check fails on it) and the same rule lives in `globals.css`. A `modulepreload` list for the sky islands was tried and gave nothing once the CSS was inline (25 ms), so it is not there.

- **Worker:** `worker/index.ts` is `main` in `wrangler.jsonc` (`nodejs_compat`). `assets.run_worker_first` is `["/mcp", "/mcp/*", "/where"]`, so only those paths reach it; every other path is served straight from `dist`. `worker/index.ts` exports the `router` of `worker/router.ts`, which sends `/where` to `where.ts` (`GET` or `HEAD`; `{ city, far, lat, lon }` from `request.cf`: the city name, `far` when the rough spot is more than 100 km from Vienna, and the spot rounded to 0.1 degree, `null` where Cloudflare has no data; no upstream fetch, no logging, no storage; `Cache-Control: private, no-store`, `X-Robots-Tag: noindex`, the security headers; other methods get 405), `/mcp` and `/mcp/*` to `mcp.ts` and passes anything else to `env.ASSETS`. `request.cf` is Cloudflare request data, not a new data source, so it needs no credit. There is no Ask panel and no `/api` route: the owner removed them, with their human check and AI binding, on 2026-10-08; do not add either back without a new decision. Bindings: `MCP_LIMITER` (rate limit, 20 per 60 s, keyed by `CF-Connecting-IP`; namespace `2001`; a namespace id is any integer you pick, unique per account, and needs no dashboard step) and `ASSETS`. `worker-configuration.d.ts` is generated by `CLOUDFLARE_LOAD_DEV_VARS_FROM_DOT_ENV=false pnpm exec wrangler types` (rerun after any binding change; without the variable wrangler copies the names in `.env.local` into the file), committed, and excluded from Prettier, ESLint and the root `tsconfig.json`; `worker/tsconfig.json` extends the root one and includes it. Errors are JSON `{ "error": code }` with `Cache-Control: no-store`. Every `/mcp` and `/where` answer carries `Strict-Transport-Security`, `X-Content-Type-Options`, `Referrer-Policy` and `Permissions-Policy` (`withSecurityHeaders` in `worker/http.ts`, the same values as the `/*` rule in `public/_headers`; no CSP, as the bodies are JSON or an event stream; `geolocation=(self)` so the bubble and the footer picker may ask for the location). Keep the two lists equal. `public/_headers` also sets `Content-Type` with `charset=utf-8` on `/llms.txt` and `/cv.json`, as both hold non-ASCII text. `facts.ts` reads `/cv.json` (zod schema, `projects` included) and `/llms.txt` from `ASSETS`, caches the promise per origin and drops it on failure, and holds the text helpers `/mcp` uses. `wrangler.jsonc` has `workers_dev: false` and `preview_urls: false`: the Worker answers on the custom domain only (see **No previews**).
- **Weather and place (owner calls 2026-10-19, sky rework; replaces the Worker `/weather` route):** only the home reads the live weather (owner pick E1, 2026-10-20); /work, /about, the blog, posts and the 404 draw one fixed calm night (`nightWeather` in `weather.ts`, the partly cloudy preset) and never ask Open-Meteo. The browser asks Open-Meteo directly (`src/lib/weather-client.ts`; `current=cloud_cover,cloud_cover_low,cloud_cover_mid,cloud_cover_high,weather_code,wind_speed_10m,wind_direction_10m,visibility,relative_humidity_2m,precipitation`, `connect-src` allows `https://api.open-meteo.com`). It asks once when the sky starts on the home on a full page load (a `ClientRouter` swap does not ask again, except the first swap to the home after a visit that began on a night page, which asks once because nothing was loaded; home to night eases the clouds to the night look and back to the report on the way home, about 3 s), once after a place change and when a hidden tab comes back after 30 minutes or more (`weatherStaleMs`); there is no `setInterval` and no fetch while the tab is hidden. The last report per rounded place is kept in `sessionStorage` for 15 minutes; the timeout is 8 s; any failure keeps `fallbackWeather` (partly cloudy) until the next start, place change or tab return. Night pages, crawlers, Save-Data and `?weather=<clear|partly|overcast|rain|snow|fog>` skip the fetch (`weatherAllowed`; `weatherGoal` picks the sky's weather target). The mapping is `skyWeather` in `src/lib/weather.ts`: `cumulus` from the low cover, `alto` from the mid cover (a higher, finer cumulus layer), `cirrus` from the high cover (thin streaks), the overcast deck when low plus mid passes 85% (compared on the raw percentages; by day it is a light grey, darker in rain, see "Grey day" under Painted sky in `.claude/rules/sky.md`), `haze` from visibility and humidity (1 on a normal day, 0.8 above 40 km, up to 2.5 at 2 km or 95%; it feeds the Mie haze of the sky light and the horizon mist), `rain` strength 0 to 1 from `precipitation` (0.1 mm per 15 minutes gives 0.3, 1 mm gives 1) and the weather code, `fog` from codes 45 and 48 or visibility under 1 km, `snow` and wind as before.
